# ConverterForAll Human Tone & Content Style Guidelines

Always maintain this natural, human, authentic writing tone across all blog articles, tool descriptions, FAQs, metadata, and UI copy:

### 1. The Core Formula
**Real Problem (Friction user faces) → Plain Explanation (Why it happens) → Simple Steps (Practical solutions) → Natural Tool Mention.**
Write like a helpful, tech-savvy friend solving an everyday computer problem, never like a marketing agency or an AI tool.

### 2. Forbidden AI Buzzwords & Marketing Jargon
- **NEVER use:** "ultimate", "next-generation", "seamlessly", "advanced", "highly sophisticated", "unparalleled", "blazing-fast", "widely considered the best", "perfect", "instant", "state-of-the-art", "drastically reduce", "cutting-edge".
- **NO ALL-CAPS shouting:** Do not use all-caps blocks like `WITHOUT COMPROMISING QUALITY`, `100% CLIENT-SIDE PRIVACY`, `ZERO UPLOAD LAG`, `YES, TOTALLY FREE`. Use calm, normal capitalization.
- **NO Corporate Mission Speak:** Avoid phrases like "Our philosophy is simple...", "Here is what makes us the ideal daily utility...".

### 3. Accurate, Standardized Privacy Framing
- **NEVER make blanket claims** like "100% private: files never leave your computer" (since certain complex conversions like Word-to-PDF use RAM-based cloud processing).
- **Always use standardized, honest phrasing:**
  > *"Most of our tools run directly inside your browser so your documents stay on your device. For complex conversions that need temporary cloud processing, files are handled in memory and deleted immediately after download."*

### 4. No Competitor Attacks
- Never claim competitors have "fake queues", "purposely throttle users", or make unverified negative claims.
- Focus purely on ConverterForAll's direct strengths: **Free, No daily limits for supported tools, No account or email required, No watermarks, In-browser processing.**

### 5. Titles Must Be Real Search Questions
- Write titles that match what humans type into Google when something is broken or needed (e.g., *"Why Is My PDF Too Large?"*, *"Old Hindi or Punjabi Text Not Working? Here's How to Convert It to Unicode"*, *"Do You Really Need to Upload Your Files to Convert Them?"*).
- Avoid template marketing titles like *"The Ultimate Complete Guide To..."*.
